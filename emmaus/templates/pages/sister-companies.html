IQ Fortune

IQ Fortune Advanced Technology PLC

Established in 2004, IQ Fortune Advanced Technology PLC is a leading distributor of computer components, printing machinery, copiers, and high-quality office consumables, including paper, toners, cartridges, and inks. Building on a strong reputation for reliability and solid financial growth, the company has since strategically expanded its operations into the agricultural export sector, specializing in premium oilseeds, pulses, and spices.

  • Printing Machinery & Copiers
  • Paper, Toners, Cartridges & Inks
  • Oilseeds, Pulses & Spices
