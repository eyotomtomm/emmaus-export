Who We Are

About Emmaus Import Export PLC

Emmaus Import Export PLC was established with a vision to become one of Ethiopia's leading agricultural export companies. We specialize in sourcing, processing, and exporting premium Ethiopian agricultural products that meet international quality standards. Our experienced team works closely with farmers, cooperatives, and suppliers to ensure consistent quality, traceability, and timely delivery. We believe in building long-term partnerships based on trust, transparency, and customer satisfaction.
