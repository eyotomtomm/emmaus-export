Home Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Emmaus Import Export PLC collects through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Who we are

Emmaus Import Export PLC, Lemi Kura Sub-city, Woreda 08, RIM SEVEN Building, House No. 801, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. You can reach us at info@emmausimportexport.com or +251 91 1192862.

What we collect

We collect information only when you choose to send it to us through an enquiry or quote form on this site. Those forms ask for:

  • your name, and your company name if you give one;
  • your email address and, optionally, your phone number;
  • the product category you are interested in;
  • whatever you write in the message field.

We do not ask for and do not want payment details, identity document numbers, or any other sensitive information through this website.

Why we collect it and what we do with it

We use it for one purpose: to read your enquiry and reply to it. Submissions are delivered by email to our sales mailbox at info@emmausimportexport.com and are handled by our own staff.

We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it with third parties for their own marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list because you sent an enquiry.

Cookies

This site sets only the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie and a security token that protects forms against cross-site request forgery. There are no advertising cookies, and no third-party analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept in our business correspondence for as long as they are relevant to a current or prospective trading relationship, and are deleted when they no longer are.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@emmausimportexport.com and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date shown above.
