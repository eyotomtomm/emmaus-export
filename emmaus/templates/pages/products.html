Frankincense (Ethiopian Etan)

Ethiopian Etan is a rare and aromatic type of frankincense derived from the Boswellia papyrifera tree, which grows naturally in the dry, mountainous regions of Ethiopia. Revered for its sweet, woody, and slightly citrusy aroma, Etan holds deep cultural, spiritual, and medicinal significance in Ethiopian tradition.

For centuries, Ethiopian communities have used Etan in religious ceremonies and traditional medicine. Today, Etan is gaining global recognition for its calming effects, anti-inflammatory properties, digestive support and skincare uses.

  • Source: Boswellia papyrifera tree
  • Harvest: 100% natural, hand-harvested
  • Sourcing: Sustainably sourced from local communities
  • Active Compounds: Rich in terpenes and boswellic acids

Myrrh

Premium Ethiopian myrrh resin, harvested from Commiphora trees native to Ethiopia's lowland regions. Valued globally for its distinctive warm, earthy aroma and wide use in perfumery, traditional medicine, and aromatherapy.

  • Source: Commiphora myrrha tree
  • Type: Natural resin
  • Harvest: Hand-harvested

Thyme (Tosign)

Ethiopian Thyme, locally known as Tosign, is an aromatic herbal leaf that organically grows in the fertile lands of Ethiopia. Carefully sun-dried and packed for consumption. Gluten-Free and Natural.

High in dietary fiber, protein, and essential vitamins and minerals. Used for its medicinal benefits — supports the immune system, reduces inflammation, and improves digestion.

  • Type: Sun-dried herbal leaf
  • Certifications: Gluten-Free, Natural
  • Benefits: Immune support, anti-inflammatory, digestive aid
